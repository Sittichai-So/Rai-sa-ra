Tiles and decorations for the /rpg/forest zone ("ป่าต้องคำสาป").

Source: "Free Topdown Fantasy - Forest - Pixelart Tileset" by aamatniekss
(aamatniekss.itch.io/topdown-fantasy-forest), a 16x16px topdown forest
tileset. Downloaded free (the pack became free on release of the author's
paid spiritual successor "Era of Fantasy: Grasslands").
License: not CC0, but explicitly free for personal and commercial use with
modification; credit appreciated but not required; redistribution/resale of
the raw assets themselves is not allowed. This is the only pack used in this
project so far that isn't CC0 — documented here for that reason.

tile_grass.png, tile_dirt.png, tile_water.png, tile_darkgrass.png — single
16x16 tiles cropped from the pack's Tileset.png sprite sheet, used as flat
ground fills (this project does not implement the pack's full Wang-tile
auto-tiling, same simplified approach as the village/dungeon ground layers).

tree_bushy.png, tree_pine.png, bush_big.png, bush_small.png, log.png,
reeds.png, boulder.png, tile_cliff.png, mushroom_cluster.png,
mushroom_single.png — decoration sprites cropped from the pack's
Decorations.png sheet, each isolated to its own tight bounding box. Used as
regular scenery (trees/bushes/boulders/cliff — the cliff crop is placed as a
decoration, not a full mountain range) as well as two of the "cursed forest"
encounter markers (haunted_grove uses a mushroom cluster since that event's
antagonist in Events.js is a bodiless whispering voice with no physical
sprite to represent; bandit_camp reuses the log as a camp-fire-adjacent
marker, since this pack has no bandit/humanoid character sprite).

wild-tileset-openrtp.png: ground tiles (grass, dark grass, dirt, ridged dirt, water and generated shorelines)
cut from the OpenRTP world tileset by finalbossblues (https://finalbossblues.itch.io/openrtp-tiles).
